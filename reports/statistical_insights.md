
# Statistical Analysis – Netflix Dataset

## 1. Movie Duration

- Mean: 99.56 minutes
- Median: 98 minutes
- Mode: 90 minutes
- Standard Deviation: 28.29
- IQR: 27 minutes

Finding:
Most Netflix movies have durations concentrated around
90–100 minutes.

## 2. TV Show Seasons

- Mean: 1.76 seasons
- Median: 1 season
- Mode: 1 season
- Maximum: 17 seasons
- Skewness: 3.42

Finding:
Most TV shows have one or two seasons, while a
smaller number have many seasons.

## 3. Release Year

- Mean: 2014
- Median: 2017
- Minimum: 1925
- Maximum: 2021

Finding:
Netflix's catalogue has a strong representation of
relatively recent content.

## 4. Distribution Analysis

Movie duration has slight positive skewness (0.20).

TV show seasons have strong positive skewness (3.42).

Finding:
TV show season counts are concentrated at lower values,
with a small number of long-running shows.

## 5. Business Implications

1. Movie duration can support time-based recommendations.
2. Season count can help analyse episodic content preferences.
3. Release year can support content discovery and filtering.
4. Rating distributions can support audience segmentation.
5. Country-level content patterns can inform regional content strategies.
